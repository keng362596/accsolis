FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Contact our team directly at info@accsolis.co.th

What is AccSolis?

AccSolis is an accounting and audit firm in Thailand with more than 20 years of experience. It provides bookkeeping, tax filing, year-end closing, financial statement audit, accounting system setup and BOI advisory to businesses nationwide, and is a certified service partner of FlowAccount.

What services does AccSolis provide?

AccSolis provides monthly bookkeeping, VAT and withholding tax filing, payroll and social security, year-end financial statements, financial statement audit and analysis, company registration, accounting system setup, and accounting and advisory services for companies promoted by Thailand's Board of Investment (BOI).

Which areas does AccSolis serve?

AccSolis serves clients in every province of Thailand. Because the firm works on an online accounting platform, it can manage documents and advise clients from anywhere, not only in Bangkok.

How are AccSolis accounting fees calculated?

Fees depend on the type of business, document volume and the scope of work required. The initial consultation is free of charge, so the team can assess your needs and propose a suitable package.

Can AccSolis handle accounting for BOI-promoted companies?

Yes. AccSolis has experience with companies promoted by Thailand's Board of Investment, including keeping separate accounts for BOI and non-BOI activities, reporting under promotion conditions, and advising on compliance with promotion certificates.

How does AccSolis work with FlowAccount?

AccSolis is a certified service partner of FlowAccount, a leading Thai online accounting platform. Clients issue documents and tax invoices and see their financial position in real time, while the AccSolis team manages their accounts in the same system.

How much experience does AccSolis have?

AccSolis has more than 20 years of experience in accounting and auditing across a wide range of industries, including BOI-promoted companies.

How do I get started with AccSolis?

Contact AccSolis via LINE Official Account, Facebook or email at info@accsolis.co.th. The team will ask about your business, assess the scope of work and plan the right accounting support. The initial consultation is free of charge.
